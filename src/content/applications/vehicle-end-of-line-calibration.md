## What you calibrate

- **Front camera** aiming and intrinsics at a fixed station.

- **Surround-view cameras**, usually with one target per camera around the vehicle.

- **LiDAR and radar alignment** in the same cell.

## What matters on a line

A station target is mounted once and used for years. It needs to stay flat as temperature changes, have a matte surface under bright station lighting, and keep its geometry so every vehicle is calibrated against the same reference.

That is why we make station targets as single, rigid pieces rather than tiled prints, and state the target geometry in the datasheet. Tell us the station layout, camera positions and lighting, and we will propose sizes and mounting.

## How we work with you

Station targets are quoted per project. Send us the drawing or pattern your station software expects, the working distance and the mounting, and we come back with a proposal and a lead time.
