## Overview

The ChArUco ceramic calibration target uses lithographic deposition of blue chrome on an alumina (Al2O3) substrate, achieving feature tolerances of +/-2 um. Designed for metrology-grade applications, semiconductor inspection, and high-accuracy stereo calibration.

## Substrate

- Material: 96% alumina ceramic (Al2O3)
- Thickness: 3 mm
- Surface: lithographed blue chrome on white ceramic
- CTE: approximately 7 um/(m.K)
- Operating temperature: -40 to +150 C
- Flatness: <= 0.05 mm across full board

## Feature accuracy

- Feature tolerance: +/-2 um (lithographic process)
- Edge definition: < 5 um
- Suitable for: stereo calibration, structured light, interferometric setups

## Storage and handling

Handle with lint-free gloves. Store in the supplied hard case. Clean with isopropanol and a lint-free cloth. Do not use abrasive materials.
