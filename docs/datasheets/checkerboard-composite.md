## Overview

The checkerboard composite calibration target provides a classic black-and-white grid pattern on a rigid aluminium composite substrate. Ideal for OpenCV camera calibration with findChessboardCorners().

## Available sizes

| Part number | Board size | Grid | Square | Inner corners | Tolerance |
|-------------|-----------|------|--------|---------------|-----------|
| CHT-P-7x10-200x150-25 | 200 x 150 mm | 7 x 10 | 25 mm | 54 | +/-0.3 mm |
| CHT-P-7x10-300x200-25 | 300 x 200 mm | 7 x 10 | 25 mm | 54 | +/-0.3 mm |
| CHT-P-9x6-400x300-30 | 400 x 300 mm | 9 x 6 | 30 mm | 40 | +/-0.3 mm |
| CHT-P-5x8-600x400-40 | 600 x 400 mm | 5 x 8 | 40 mm | 28 | +/-0.3 mm |

## Substrate

- Material: aluminium / LDPE composite (Dibond-type), 6 mm thick
- Surface: UV-printed, matte finish, scratch-resistant
- CTE: approximately 24 um/(m.K)
- Operating temperature: -50 to +80 C

## Feature accuracy

- Feature tolerance: +/-0.3 mm + 0.2 mm/m
- Flatness: <= 0.5 mm/m
- Corner radius: 6 mm

## Deliverables

Each target ships with:

- Dimensional inspection report (included)
- Datasheet PDF
- DXF board outline
- OpenCV JSON configuration

## Storage and handling

Store flat, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Clean with a soft, dry cloth.
